Shows Now Watching, percent watched, and time left on the bottom-left overlay. It stays visible while paused. After resume it hides unless you are hovering the player. Click the card to open the title on Simkl. No top-left popup.

OSD message length (seconds)

How long remaining top-left OSD messages stay on screen, such as Skip Intro confirmations. Range 1–15 seconds.

Show titles in

English uses Simkl’s English title when available, for example From Overshadowed to Overpowered: Second Reincarnation of a Talentless Sage. Original uses the catalog title, for example Rakudai Kenja no Gakuin Musou: Nidome no Tensei, S Rank Cheat Majutsushi Bouken Roku.

Draws a poster card on the video when a new episode starts. This needs the Video Overlay permission (Settings → this plugin → Permissions). Use Plugin → Preview Now Playing Overlay to test it.

Overlay display length (seconds)

How long the poster card stays fully visible when an episode starts. Hover anywhere in the player after it fades to show it again. Range 1–30 seconds.

Overlay length after resume (seconds)

After you unpause, hide the poster card in this many seconds unless the pointer is over the player. Hovering keeps it visible. Range 1–30 seconds.

If IntroDB has nothing for the file, OP / ED / recap / preview chapters in the file are used instead. The button stays for the whole window, fades after 5 seconds, and remains clickable until that segment ends. Needs the Video Overlay permission.

Off by default. When on, a stop at 80% or more of a title you already finished is logged as a separate viewing. Never sent on play or pause. Requires Simkl Pro or VIP; free accounts are ignored.

Pause debounce (ms)

Ignore brief pauses caused by seeking. Real pauses and resumes are sent to Simkl (Now Watching pauses, then continues).
